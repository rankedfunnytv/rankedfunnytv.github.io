Privacy Policy – Ranked Funny Uploader

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Ranked Funny Uploader is a private application used solely by the owner of the Ranked Funny YouTube channel (@RankedFunnyTV) to upload and manage videos on that channel.

Data we access

With the account owner's consent, the app uses Google OAuth to access the YouTube Data API in order to upload videos, set thumbnails and read basic channel information for the owner's own channel.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from any other users.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
